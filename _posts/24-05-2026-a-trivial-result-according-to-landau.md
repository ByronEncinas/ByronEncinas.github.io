# A Trivial Result According to Landau

I spent the first year of my masters degree studying what I could on theoretical physics, so eventually and as expected I gravitated towards the Landau & Lifshitz Graduate Textbooks. Thankfully I had friends willing to go over the first volume and solve ALL of the problems. Of course, we didn't completed all the problems, but most of them. We also derive all equations and results that seemed non-trivial. Then, on page 28 this appeared.


> $$\int_U^a \frac{dE}{\sqrt{E-U} \sqrt{a-E}}$$
> 
> The integral over E is elementary; its value is $\pi$.

In retrospect, the form of this integral, does not seem unsolvable. But it is also not trivial, and it is probably (one of) the first result(s) that make the Landau books difficult. 

It is not my intention to solve the equation here, because I have noticed that the these books must be read carefully and each chapter gives you just enough tools to derive everything by yourself.
