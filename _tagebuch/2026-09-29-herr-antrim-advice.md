# Herr Antrim Advice to learn using content.

Consuming in the goal language can be used more effectively if the tasks or study session become more conscious. Instead of flooding the brain with 8 seasons of a show in German and Spanish subs, or Spanish with German subs, one can select a relevant clip and make it into a full fledge lesson. 

The steps according to Herr Antrim are as follows.

1. Choose a goal

Is there something relevant that I could focus on in this lesson? Maybe gain more vocabulary, sentence structure, grammar in real life situations, etc

2. Experience the content

What is the content talking about? Is there some specific part of it that matches your goal? Maybe the subject is too complex and so the need to look for something according to your level. There is definitely a segment that if isolated, could be useful either way. Short form content might be more helpful than a 20 minute episode of tageschau.

3. Investigate what matters

New words, sentence patterns (choose), grammar, cases, there can be lots of details worth focusing. Subtitles can help connect sound to written form, may help hearing comprehension, etc. It is not the only answer! Don't care if you miss something, hearing and reading german on german subtitles can be good to focus on the language. This is why it is many times recommended to try first with a piece of content that is familiar to you. My personal favorite is watching Dr. House in German with German subs. 

4. Use the German

Don't store your findings, put them into practice. Try that new sentence structure, use that new word, use it in a new example. Practice recording yourself, anything that results in creating something new is worthwhile.

6. Return to original content

See how much do you understand or identify! Something might come up that you didn't catch before. Can I anticipate what is going to be said? Remember, the goal is not to understand completely, but to understand something more! Measure your progress and look for more!
